Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Moon & Whisk website and the Moon & Whisk Pin Scheduler, a small personal tool that publishes pins to the Moon & Whisk Pinterest account.

This website

This site has no accounts, sign-ups, forms, cookies, analytics or ads. The free sample download needs no email. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Moon & Whisk Pin Scheduler

The Pin Scheduler is used only by Moon & Whisk, on its own Pinterest account. Through Pinterest's official API it:

  • reads the names of Moon & Whisk's own boards, so it knows where to publish, and
  • creates pins on those boards showing pages from Moon & Whisk's books.

It does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user. The API access token stays on the owner's own computer and is never shared. Nothing is sold or passed to third parties.

Books bought on Amazon

Purchases happen on Amazon and are covered by Amazon's own privacy notice. Moon & Whisk does not receive buyers' personal information.
